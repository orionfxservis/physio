What We Treat

Comprehensive Physiotherapy Services

From acute injury rehabilitation to long-term chronic condition management — evidence-based care at every stage.

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Specialised protocols for athletes recovering from ligament, tendon, and muscle injuries with performance-focused goals.

Post-Surgical Recovery

Structured post-operative care following orthopaedic, spinal, and joint replacement surgeries to restore full function.

Spinal & Posture Correction

Assessment and treatment of scoliosis, kyphosis, disc herniation, sciatica, and chronic back and neck conditions.

Chronic Pain Management

Multimodal approach combining manual therapy, exercise, and pain neuroscience education for lasting relief.

Paediatric Physiotherapy

Child-centred rehabilitation for developmental conditions, cerebral palsy, torticollis, and paediatric injuries.

Neurological Rehabilitation

Specialist programmes for stroke recovery, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, and vestibular disorders.
